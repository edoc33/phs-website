## The Short Answer

Most Mississauga homes need a deep clean every 3 to 6 months, with regular cleaning in between. But how often really depends on your household.

## What Counts as a Deep Clean?

A deep clean goes beyond your regular tidying. It includes things like:

- Scrubbing inside the oven, fridge, and dishwasher
- Cleaning baseboards, light switches, and door frames
- Washing windows and window tracks
- Cleaning behind and under furniture
- Scrubbing bathroom grout and fixtures
- Dusting ceiling fans, vents, and light fixtures

This is the kind of cleaning that resets your home. If you are not sure [what a professional deep clean includes](/journal/what-professional-cleaning-includes), the scope goes well beyond regular tidying.

## It Depends on Your Home

### Household size

A couple in a Lorne Park condo and a family of five in Churchill Meadows have very different cleaning needs. More people in the house means things get dirty faster.

- **1-2 people:** Every 4-6 months
- **3-4 people:** Every 3-4 months
- **5+ people:** Every 2-3 months

### Pets

If you have dogs or cats, hair and dander build up fast. It gets into the upholstery, under the furniture, and into every corner. Pet owners in Mississauga should plan on a deep clean every 2-3 months. If dogs are the main culprit, our guide to [cleaning a home with dogs](/journal/cleaning-home-with-dogs) covers the daily habits that help between deep cleans.

### Allergies

Mississauga's spring pollen season hits hard, especially near the Credit River and lakefront areas like Port Credit. If anyone in your home has allergies or asthma, a deep clean every 3 months helps keep things under control.

### Home age and type

Older homes in Streetsville or Clarkson tend to get dustier. Older furnaces and less insulation. Newer places in Erin Mills or Square One condos stay cleaner longer, but smaller spaces show messes faster.

## A Practical Schedule

Here's what works for most Mississauga families:

- **Weekly or bi-weekly:** Regular cleaning (surfaces, floors, bathrooms, kitchen)
- **Every 3-4 months:** Full deep clean
- **Twice a year:** Seasonal deep clean (spring and fall) that includes windows, closets, and outdoor spaces

## When to Bring in Help

Deep cleaning takes time. A proper job on a 3-bedroom home is 4-6 hours if you do it yourself. Someone who does this every day will be faster and won't miss the spots you'd walk right past.
