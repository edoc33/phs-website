If your air fryer basket looks like a crime scene, you're not alone. These appliances are used hard, and cleaning them is one of those jobs people avoid until it becomes urgent.

A few tricks have gone viral lately. The most popular one involves putting a wet paper towel in the basket and running the air fryer for a few minutes to steam the grease loose. Let's talk about what works and what just moves the mess around.

## The Steam Trick

It does loosen fresh grease. If you run it right after cooking, while things are still warm, the steam helps lift residue from the basket. That part is real.

The problem is what happens next. You still have to scrub it. The steam doesn't remove the buildup. It softens it. People try the hack, think it didn't work, and give up. The trick is to follow it immediately with a proper wash.

Also, paper towels can stick or tear inside a hot appliance. Use a small damp cloth if you're going to try this at all.

## What Actually Gets an Air Fryer Clean

**Soak the basket**: Pull it out and soak it in hot soapy water for 10 to 15 minutes. This is the step most people skip, and it makes everything easier.

**Soft brush or old toothbrush**: The mesh on most baskets traps grease in the holes. A toothbrush gets into those spots without scratching the coating.

**Baking soda paste for stubborn spots**: Mix baking soda with a little dish soap into a paste. Apply it, let it sit for 5 minutes, then scrub. It cuts through baked-on grease without harsh chemicals.

**The drawer under the basket**: This one gets ignored constantly. Grease drips down there every time you cook. Pull it out and wash it the same way as the basket.

**The heating element**: Turn the unit upside down. You'll often find splattered grease on the element itself. Wipe it gently with a damp cloth when the unit is fully cool and unplugged. Don't soak it. Don't spray cleaner directly on it.

**Dry everything completely**: Before you put it back together, make sure the basket and drawer are fully dry. Putting them back wet leads to that musty smell people complain about.

## How Often Should You Clean It

After every few uses, give the basket and drawer a proper wash. Once a month, do the full clean including the heating element and the inside walls of the unit. If you cook fatty foods like bacon or chicken thighs regularly, do it more often.

A greasy air fryer doesn't just smell. It smokes. That smoke sets off alarms and coats the inside of the appliance with another layer of buildup that's harder to remove each time.

## The Hacks Worth Keeping

The internet isn't wrong about everything. A few things that genuinely help:

**Parchment liners**: The perforated paper liners made for air fryers catch a lot of the drip before it reaches the basket. They don't work for everything, but for messy foods they're worth using.

**Dish soap in the basket before soaking**: Don't just fill it with water. Add a good squeeze of dish soap first. It starts cutting the grease while it soaks.

**Clean it warm, not hot**: Give it 10 minutes after cooking before you take it apart. Warm is easier to clean than cold. Hot is just a burn risk.

The steam hack isn't useless. It's just incomplete. Most viral cleaning tricks have a grain of truth in them. The problem is the videos end before the actual scrubbing starts.
