Air fryers get dirty fast. Grease bakes onto the basket, the drawer smells like last Tuesday's fish sticks, and the inside of the unit collects splatter most people never think about. A few TikTok videos promise a quick fix. Let's go through what holds up and what doesn't.

## The Viral Hack

The method making the rounds: fill the air fryer basket with hot water and a few drops of dish soap, run it at 200°F for a few minutes, then wipe it out. Some versions add baking soda.

It works, but only for light grease. If your basket has a thin film of oil from last night's fries, yes, the heat loosens it and the wipe-down is easy. If you haven't cleaned it in two months, this will disappoint you.

## What Actually Gets an Air Fryer Clean

**The basket and drawer**: These come out. Soak them in hot soapy water for 15 to 20 minutes. Use a soft brush or an old toothbrush on the mesh. Don't use steel wool or anything abrasive — it scratches the non-stick coating and then food sticks worse than before.

**The heating coil**: Unplug the unit first. Flip it upside down. The coil sits at the top inside the machine. Wipe it gently with a damp cloth. If there's caked grease, a soft toothbrush with a little baking soda paste works well. Be careful not to bend anything.

**The inside walls**: A damp cloth with a small amount of dish soap is enough for most of it. Dry it thoroughly before you use it again.

**The outside**: Wipe it down with a damp cloth. That's it.

## Things That Actually Cause Problems

Putting the basket in the dishwasher sounds convenient. Some manufacturers say it's fine. In practice, repeated dishwasher cycles break down the non-stick coating faster. Hand washing takes three minutes and the basket lasts much longer.

Running the hot water trick when there's a lot of built-up grease just steams the grease and redeposits it. You end up with a greasy film on the inside walls that smells when you run it again.

Lemon juice in the water is another popular addition. It doesn't hurt, but it doesn't do much either. The heat and soap are doing the work.

## How Often to Clean It

After every use: wipe the basket and drawer. Takes a minute.

Once a week if you use it regularly: full wash of the basket and drawer, quick wipe of the interior.

Once a month: flip it over and check the coil. A quick pass with a damp cloth keeps it from building up.

If it smells when you run it, it needs a deeper clean. The smell comes from grease burning on the coil or the interior walls. Running it at high heat to "burn off" the smell just bakes the grease in further. Clean it first, then run it.

## One More Thing

If your air fryer basket has lost its non-stick coating and food is sticking no matter what you do, cleaning it more won't help. The coating is gone. It's time for a new basket, or a new unit. Continuing to use a scratched non-stick surface isn't great for your food.
