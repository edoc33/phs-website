Air fryers are easy to use and surprisingly hard to clean properly. The basket gets greasy fast, the coating scratches easily, and a lot of the advice floating around online will cause more damage than the grease did.

Here is what works.

## Why Air Fryers Get So Grimy

The hot circulating air pushes grease and food particles into every corner of the basket and drawer. If you do not clean it after every few uses, the buildup bakes on harder each time. By the time most people notice the smell, it has been sitting there for weeks.

## What to Do After Each Use

Let the basket cool completely before touching it. Hot non-stick coatings are more vulnerable to scratching. Once it is cool, wipe out any loose crumbs and give the basket a quick wash with warm water and dish soap. That is all it needs if you do it regularly.

## The Deep Clean (Every Few Weeks)

**Soak the basket**: Fill your sink with hot water and a few drops of dish soap. Submerge the basket and drawer for 20 to 30 minutes. Most of the grease will loosen on its own.

**Use a soft brush**: A silicone brush or an old soft toothbrush gets into the mesh without scratching. Avoid anything abrasive, including the rough side of a sponge.

**Wipe the interior**: Unplug the unit. Use a damp cloth to wipe the inside walls and the heating element at the top. A little baking soda paste works well on stubborn spots up there.

**Dry everything fully**: Before reassembling, make sure every piece is completely dry. Moisture near the heating element is a problem.

## What NOT to Do

**Skip the dishwasher**: Most manufacturers say the basket is dishwasher safe. That does not mean it is a good idea. The harsh detergent and high heat wear down non-stick coatings faster than hand washing. After enough dishwasher cycles, food starts sticking more and more.

**Do not use steel wool or abrasive scrubbers**: The non-stick coating on most air fryer baskets is thin. Scratch it and you are cooking on damaged coating.

**Do not spray water directly inside the unit**: The main body is an electrical appliance. Wipe it down with a damp cloth, not a running tap.

**Avoid oven cleaner sprays**: These are too harsh for the coating and the plastic components. Dish soap and baking soda are enough.

## The TikTok Hacks Worth Skipping

There is a popular trick where you fill the basket with soapy water, run the air fryer for a few minutes, and let it "self-clean." It gets a lot of views. It is also a good way to push water into electrical components and shorten the life of your appliance. Do not try it.

Another common one involves lining the basket with foil or parchment while cooking to catch grease. Parchment designed for air fryers is fine. Regular foil placed flat across the basket blocks airflow and can be a fire risk. If you use foil, only line the sides, never the bottom.

## When the Smell Does Not Go Away

If your air fryer still smells like old grease after a thorough clean, cut a lemon in half and rub it around the inside of the basket. Leave it for 10 minutes, then rinse. The acid cuts through residual grease and neutralizes odour without any harsh chemicals.

For really persistent smells, a bowl of baking soda left inside the closed (unplugged) unit overnight will absorb a lot of it.

## One Last Thing

An air fryer is not much different from any other appliance in your kitchen. Clean it regularly and it will last. Ignore it and the grease builds up until nothing really cleans it off anymore.
