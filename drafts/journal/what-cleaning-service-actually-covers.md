A recent poll found that cleaning and laundry are the two household chores Canadians most want to hand off to someone else. That tracks with what I hear from clients. People are tired, and those two jobs never seem to end.

If you're thinking about hiring a cleaning service, it helps to know what's actually included before you call anyone.

## What a Regular House Cleaning Usually Covers

Most professional home cleaning services handle the following on a regular visit:

**Kitchen**: Wiping counters, stovetop, exterior of appliances, cabinet fronts, sink, and floors.

**Bathrooms**: Scrubbing toilets, sinks, tubs, showers, mirrors, and floors.

**Bedrooms and living areas**: Dusting surfaces, cleaning mirrors, vacuuming, and mopping floors.

**General tidying**: Emptying small wastebaskets, straightening surfaces if clutter isn't excessive.

This is what a standard recurring clean covers. A first-time or deep clean will go further: baseboards, interior of appliances, inside cabinets, window sills, and so on.

## What Most Cleaning Services Don't Do

**Laundry**: Most house cleaning services don't do laundry. Some will switch a load or fold what's already clean if you ask and it's arranged ahead of time. But it's not standard. If laundry is your main pain point, you'd need a service that specifically offers it, or a housekeeper who is hired for a broader scope of household tasks.

**Dishes**: Some cleaners will load a dishwasher or clear a sink as part of the kitchen clean. A large pile of dishes is usually outside the regular scope.

**Organizing**: Cleaning and organizing are different jobs. A cleaner will clean around clutter but typically won't sort through it, file papers, or reorganize shelves. That's a separate service.

**Childcare or errand running**: Not part of the job, ever.

## What to Ask Before You Book

If you're comparing services, these questions save a lot of confusion later:

**What's included in the base price?** Get a clear list. Vague answers lead to disappointment.

**Do I get the same cleaner each time?** This matters more than most people expect. A cleaner who knows your home cleans it faster and better over time.

**What's your cancellation policy?** Life happens. Make sure you know the terms.

**Do you bring your own supplies?** Some services do, some expect you to provide products. Confirm this upfront.

**What happens if something's missed?** A reliable company will come back and fix it. Ask how they handle that.

## The Real Question Behind the Poll

The poll isn't just about chores. It's about time. Most people who hire a cleaner aren't lazy. They're working, raising kids, caring for parents, or simply running out of hours in the week.

Spending two or three hours on Saturday scrubbing a bathroom is time you don't get back. For a lot of families, having a cleaner come every two weeks changes the whole feel of the weekend.
