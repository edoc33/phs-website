Most people clean what they can see. The floor gets mopped. The sink gets scrubbed. But there are surfaces in every home that almost never get touched, and over time they quietly collect dust, grease, and grime.

Here are the ones we find most often when we come into a home for the first time.

## The tops of kitchen cabinets

If there's a gap between your cabinets and the ceiling, that ledge is collecting grease and dust every single day. Cooking fumes rise. The grease lands up there and gets sticky. Then the dust sticks to the grease. After a year or two, it's a solid layer.

Wipe it down with warm soapy water and a cloth. Do it twice a year at minimum. Once you see what comes off, you won't forget again.

## The light switches and outlet covers

People touch these dozens of times a day. Almost nobody cleans them. A damp cloth with a bit of dish soap takes thirty seconds per plate. Unscrew the covers once a year and get around the edges too.

## Underneath and behind the refrigerator

The coils at the back collect dust. So does the floor underneath. That dust makes your fridge work harder and run warmer. Pull it out once a year. Vacuum the coils. Wipe the floor.

It also catches crumbs, bottle caps, and the occasional small toy.

## The inside of the microwave door seal

The glass gets wiped. The turntable gets removed and washed. But the rubber seal around the door? It traps food splatter and moisture. Mold can start there quietly. Run a damp cloth around the seal every couple of weeks.

## Curtain rods and the tops of door frames

Both sit at eye level when you're standing on a ladder and well above it when you're not. Dust settles there in thick layers. A damp microfibre cloth does the job. Do it when you're already cleaning the room and you won't need a separate session.

## The bathroom exhaust fan cover

This one surprises people. The plastic grille collects lint and dust until it looks grey. When it's clogged, the fan can't pull moisture out of the room. That moisture has to go somewhere, and it usually goes into your walls or ceiling. Pop the cover off, wash it with soap and water, let it dry, and put it back. Takes five minutes.

## Knobs, pulls, and handles on every drawer and cabinet

Kitchen, bathroom, bedroom. All of them. They get touched constantly and cleaned almost never. Soap and water, or a mild all-purpose spray. This is especially worth doing if someone in the house has been sick.

## The washing machine drum and seal

Yes, the thing that cleans your clothes needs to be cleaned. Front-loaders especially. The rubber gasket around the door traps water and lint and gets musty fast. Wipe it dry after every load. Once a month, run a hot cycle with a cup of white vinegar and nothing else.
