Burnt-on grease in an air fryer is one of those things people ignore until it starts smoking. Then they panic and grab whatever is nearby. That's usually where the damage happens.

Here's how to clean it properly.

## First, let it cool completely

Never clean a hot air fryer. The basket is often non-stick coated, and hot coated surfaces scratch more easily. Give it at least 30 minutes after use.

## What you need

**Dish soap and warm water**: This does most of the work. No need for anything stronger on a regular basis.

**Soft sponge or cloth**: Non-scratch only. Steel wool will strip the coating in one go.

**Baking soda paste**: Mix baking soda with a small amount of water to make a thick paste. Good for stubborn baked-on spots.

**Soft-bristle brush**: A clean toothbrush or small dish brush works well for the basket mesh.

**Damp cloth**: For wiping the inside of the main unit.

## The basket and tray

Remove the basket and tray and wash them in warm soapy water. Most are safe to soak for 10 to 15 minutes if there's heavy buildup. Use the baking soda paste on any spots that don't lift easily. Scrub gently with the soft brush, rinse well, and dry completely before putting back.

Some baskets are dishwasher-safe. Check your manual. If yours is, put it on the top rack only.

## The inside of the unit

Never submerge the main unit in water. Ever.

Wipe the inside walls with a damp cloth. If there's grease on the heating element at the top, let the unit cool completely, then wipe gently with a barely damp cloth. Dry it right after.

If there's a lot of splattered grease up near the element, turn the unit upside down once it's cool. That gives you better access. Wipe carefully, don't scrub hard near the element itself.

## The outside

A damp cloth and a small amount of dish soap is all you need. Dry it off after.

## What the TikTok hack gets wrong

The popular TikTok method involves filling the basket with dish soap and water and running the fryer. The idea is that the heat loosens the grease while the soapy water splashes around inside.

The problem is this sends soapy water onto the heating element. That can cause damage over time. It also doesn't do a better job than a simple soak and scrub. Grease lifts more easily with a bit of dwell time in warm water than with a short hot cycle.

Skip that one.

## How often should you clean it

After every use, wipe out any crumbs from the basket and tray. A quick rinse helps too if there was anything saucy or fatty.

Do a proper wash of the basket and tray every two or three uses. Clean the inside of the unit once a month, or sooner if you notice smoke or smell.

## A quick note on build-up

If grease has built up inside the unit over a long time, baked-on carbon residue can be harder to shift. At that point, soak the basket overnight in hot soapy water and repeat the baking soda treatment two or three times. The inside of the unit may need a few rounds of gentle wiping over a few days.

It's worth the effort. A dirty air fryer runs less efficiently and the smell transfers to your food.
