Air fryers are in almost every kitchen now. And with that comes a lot of baked-on grease, burnt crumbs, and questions about how to clean them properly.

TikTok has answers, of course. The problem is that some of those answers can damage your appliance or worse, create a safety hazard in your home.

## The Hack That's Making the Rounds

The trending trick involves spraying the air fryer basket and interior with oven cleaner, sealing it in a bag, and letting it sit overnight.

Oven cleaner contains sodium hydroxide. It is a strong caustic chemical. It works well inside an actual oven, which is built to handle it. Air fryers are not. The heating element and the non-stick coating on the basket are not designed to be exposed to that kind of chemical.

Yahoo Lifestyle Canada reported that cleaning experts have flagged this as genuinely hazardous, not just ineffective. Residue can remain on the basket and heat up the next time you use the appliance. That is not a risk worth taking over a greasy basket.

## What Actually Works

I have been cleaning homes for over 30 years. Kitchens are where most of the mess lives. Here is what I would do with a dirty air fryer:

**Let it cool first**: Never clean it hot. Give it at least 30 minutes after use.

**Soak the basket**: Remove it and soak in hot soapy water for 15 to 20 minutes. Dish soap is enough for most messes.

**Use a soft brush**: An old toothbrush or a soft-bristle brush gets into the mesh without scratching the coating. Avoid metal scrubbers. They strip non-stick surfaces.

**Wipe the interior with a damp cloth**: A little dish soap on a cloth or sponge handles the inside walls. Make sure you wring it out well. You do not want water near the heating element.

**Baking soda paste for stubborn spots**: Mix baking soda and a little water into a paste. Apply it to baked-on grease and let it sit for 10 minutes. Scrub gently, then wipe clean.

**Dry everything before reassembling**: Cloth-dry the basket and let the interior air out before putting it back together.

## A Word on Non-Stick Coatings

Once you scratch the non-stick coating, it does not come back. Harsh chemicals and abrasive tools speed that up. When the coating starts flaking, you are cooking with it, which is its own problem.

Gentle cleaning done consistently is better than aggressive cleaning done once in a while.

## How Often Should You Clean It?

After every use, shake out the crumbs and wipe the basket. A full soak and scrub every three to five uses, depending on what you cook. If you make a lot of breaded or saucy food, clean it more often. Grease builds fast and is much harder to remove once it has baked on multiple times.

## The Broader Point About Cleaning Hacks

Not every shortcut is a good one. Some of the most-viewed cleaning videos on TikTok have millions of views and are genuinely wrong. The logic of "it got the grime off in the video" ignores what that chemical is doing to the surface underneath.

Before you try a hack on an appliance you use for food, it is worth asking: is this safe, and is this surface designed to handle it?

If you are not sure, plain hot water, dish soap, and a soft cloth will handle most kitchen cleaning without any risk.
