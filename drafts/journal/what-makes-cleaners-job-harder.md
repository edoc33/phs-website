A recent article out of Toronto talked to condo cleaners about the stress and frustration that comes with the job. A lot of it rang true. Not because cleaning is a complaint, but because there are things that genuinely make it harder to do good work, and homeowners often don't realize it.

This isn't about venting. It's practical. If you want a better result, some of this will help.

## Clutter Is Not the Same as Mess

We clean. We don't sort. If counters, floors, and surfaces are covered in items that need to be moved before we can reach what's underneath, that time comes out of the cleaning time. We can only work with what we can access.

Before a visit, take ten minutes to clear surfaces you want cleaned. It makes a real difference.

## Pets Are Fine. Surprises Are Not.

Most of us have cleaned around dogs and cats for years. What's harder is not knowing they're there. If you have a pet that's anxious around strangers or tends to be underfoot, let us know in advance. We'll plan around it. An unexpected dog behind a closed door is a startled dog, and that's not good for anyone.

## "Just a Quick Clean" Covers a Lot of Ground

When someone says the place just needs a quick tidy, they often mean something different than what I mean. A bathroom that hasn't been scrubbed in two months takes longer than one cleaned weekly. That's not a judgment. It's just time.

If it's been a while, say so. We'll plan accordingly and give you an honest time estimate rather than rush through and do a poor job.

## The Temperature in the Building Matters

This sounds small, but it isn't. Cleaning a condo or house in July with no air conditioning running is hard physical work. We're scrubbing, lifting, kneeling, and moving continuously for two to four hours. If you're able to have the space reasonably cool before we arrive, it helps us work at full capacity the whole time. A warm space slows everything down.

## Instructions Left in a Note Are Better Than Instructions Left After

If there's something specific you want done differently, a note left before the visit works much better than a message sent during or a correction given after. We follow what we're told. But we can only follow instructions we have.

## Cancellations Happen. Short Notice Is Hard.

Things come up. We understand that. But a cancellation the morning of a scheduled visit means a block of time that can't be filled and income that's lost for the day. If you know in advance you need to cancel, the earlier the better. Most cleaners will remember that consideration.

## What Respect Looks Like

It doesn't take much. A clear space to work. A heads-up if something's changed. Basic communication about what you need. That's it.

The best clients we've had over thirty years aren't necessarily the ones with the tidiest homes. They're the ones who treat us like people doing a skilled job, because that's what we are.
