Ovens and air fryers get ignored until they start smoking. Then people panic and search TikTok. Some of what they find is fine. Some of it is not.

Here is a straightforward breakdown of what works and what to skip.

## Ovens

**The baking soda paste method**: This one is legitimate. Mix baking soda with just enough water to make a spreadable paste. Coat the inside of the oven, avoid the heating elements, and leave it overnight. Wipe it off in the morning. For stubborn spots, a little white vinegar on the cloth will help it lift. It takes patience but it does not damage anything.

**Dryer sheets soaked in water**: This circulates on social media. It does loosen some baked-on grease when left overnight on the oven bottom. It is not a deep clean, but it is useful for light maintenance between proper cleans.

**Self-clean mode for heavy buildup**: The oven's built-in self-clean cycle runs at very high heat and turns residue to ash. It works, but it creates a lot of smoke and fumes. Open windows. Run the range hood fan. Do not do this on a hot summer day with the windows closed. Let the oven cool completely before you wipe out the ash.

**What to avoid**: Anything abrasive on the oven window. Steel wool, rough scrubbing pads, and coarse powders scratch the glass and the enamel lining. Once the glass is scratched, it clouds permanently. Dish soap directly on the inside of the oven is also unnecessary and hard to rinse out.

## Air Fryers

The air fryer hack getting the most attention right now involves putting a small dish of water and dish soap in the basket and running it at low heat. Some people swear by it. I would not bother.

Here is what actually works.

**Wash the basket and tray after every use**: Hot water, dish soap, a soft cloth or sponge. That is all. If you let grease build up over five or six uses, nothing is going to feel like an easy fix.

**For baked-on grease in the basket**: Soak it in hot soapy water for 20 minutes. Most of it will come off with a soft brush after that. A paste of baking soda and water on the stubborn spots, left for 10 minutes, usually handles the rest.

**The heating element at the top**: This is what most people forget. Unplug the unit and let it cool. Wipe the element gently with a damp cloth. Do not submerge it or spray it directly. Grease that builds up there is what causes smoke and smell during cooking.

**Non-stick coating**: Air fryer baskets usually have a non-stick surface. Steel wool will strip it. Use a soft brush or a non-scratch sponge only. Once the coating is gone, food sticks and cleaning becomes much harder.

## The Part Nobody Talks About

Both appliances have the same problem. People clean the visible parts and ignore the inside corners, the door hinges on ovens, and the outer housing on air fryers. Grease collects in those spots and either smells over time or attracts insects during warm weather.

A quick wipe-down of the exterior with a damp cloth and a drop of dish soap every week takes 90 seconds. It keeps those areas from becoming a project.

## One More Thing on Viral Hacks

The hacks that spread the fastest are the ones that look satisfying in a 30-second video. That does not mean they are practical or safe for your specific appliance. Always check your appliance manual before trying something new. Manufacturers list what cleaners and tools void the warranty or damage the finish.
